About

Hello, my name is Antony Bridle and I am a multi-disciplined freelance graphic designer based on the Hampshire/Dorset border on the sunny south coast of the UK.

I have a combined 13 years of agency, in-house, and freelance creative experience. I've worked with clients of all sizes, from startups to international brands, on projects ranging from brand creation and web design to interior graphics and print design. Some of the clients I have had the pleasure of working with include the University of Southampton, Manchester City Football Club, Aston Martin, and DP World.

I love teaming up with talented web developers, photographers, illustrators, and copywriters to bring your vision to life and deliver amazing projects.

If you're looking for eye-catching communication design, a unique brand identity, or a dynamic website that really works, I'd love to help!
