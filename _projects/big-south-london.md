As Senior Graphic Designer at Recognition Design and Marketing I have been involved in ongoing work with BIG South London (Business Innovation and Growth), a dynamic program dedicated to fostering business growth in South London, I have been deeply involved from its inception. My contributions span from initial brand development to ongoing creative support, including website design, creating print materials, crafting event stands, producing large-scale signage, and editing promotional videos.
