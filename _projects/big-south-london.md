As Senior Graphic Designer at Recognition Design and Marketing, I worked on ongoing projects for BIG South London (Business Innovation and Growth), a dynamic programme dedicated to fostering business growth in South London.

My contributions span from initial brand development to ongoing creative support, including website design, creating print materials, crafting event stands, producing large-scale signage, and editing promotional videos.
